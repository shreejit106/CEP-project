StayMate PICT

Hyperlocal Student Housing & Roommate Matching Platform for PICT Pune

🎯 The Vision

To eliminate broker exploitation and create a unified, transparent platform connecting engineering students with authentic local hostels and managed co-living spaces.

📍 Target Hub

Dhankawadi, Katraj, Trimurti Chowk & Bharati Vidyapeeth campus belt — Pune Institute of Computer Technology (PICT).

🗣️ Speaker Script: "Good morning. Every year, freshers admitted to PICT struggle to find safe, affordable housing and compatible roommates. Today we present StayMate PICT, a purpose-built platform solving this exact problem."
The Problem Statement

The Student Housing Crisis in Pune

Real challenges faced by outstation students and parents

💸 Brokerage & Hidden Costs

Local middlemen charge 1-2 months rent (₹10,000–₹25,000) as commission for basic listings, exploiting outstation families.

📍 Misleading Distance Claims

Posters claim "2 mins from college", while in reality hostels are 2-3 km away, causing attendance issues for 8 AM practicals.

🍲 Food & Hygiene Disconnect

Students face inconsistent mess quality, hidden water/electricity charges, and unverified Wi-Fi reliability right before exams.

🤝 Incompatible Roommate Allocation

Random allocation leads to friction between Night Owl coders and Early Bird students, causing stress and mid-term room shifting.

🗣️ Speaker Script: "Students arrive in Pune and face an unorganized market with heavy brokerage, misleading distance claims, and random roommate allocation that hurts their academic routine."
